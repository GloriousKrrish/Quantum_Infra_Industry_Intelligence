# 3. How We Actually Integrate Quantum Mechanics

I want to make an important scientific distinction.

**We cannot meaningfully use quantum mechanics in every business operation.**

For example, property registration, sending quotations, calculating GST or managing contractor attendance do not need quantum mechanics.

However, we can use quantum mechanics as the foundation for specialized quantum-computing components throughout the ecosystem.

Here is how I would integrate it.

| Application                      | Quantum technology           | Purpose                                       |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Land selection                   | QAOA                         | Multi-constraint land selection optimization. |
| Supplier selection               | QAOA                         | Optimize supplier combinations.               |
| Construction scheduling          | QAOA                         | Scheduling optimization.                      |
| Workforce allocation             | Quantum optimization         | Resource allocation experiments.              |
| Financial portfolio optimization | QAOA                         | Investment allocation.                        |
| Infrastructure distribution      | QAOA                         | Resource distribution optimization.           |
| Molecular material research      | VQE                          | Molecular ground-state energy estimation.     |
| Quantum ML                       | Variational Quantum Circuits | Experimental classification and prediction.   |
| Quantum simulation               | Qiskit Aer                   | Quantum state and circuit simulation.         |

### A particularly interesting example

Suppose a developer has shortlisted 20 land parcels.

They want to select the best combination considering:

* Total budget.
* Land size.
* Location.
* Infrastructure.
* Expected returns.
* Environmental constraints.

We can formulate this as a constrained optimization problem.

Our platform can compare:

1. Classical optimization.
2. Genetic algorithms.
3. Simulated annealing.
4. Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA).

It can then show which approach provides the best solution under the experimental conditions.

We should not claim quantum superiority unless the benchmarks actually establish it.

### Quantum Mechanics Research Component

To give Quantum Mechanics a genuine role, I would also include a dedicated scientific simulation laboratory:

* Qubit state visualization.
* Bloch sphere visualization.
* Superposition experiments.
* Entanglement experiments.
* Quantum measurement.
* Hamiltonian construction.
* Schrödinger equation demonstrations.
* VQE molecular energy experiments.

This makes the project academically meaningful rather than just attaching Qiskit to a real estate application.
